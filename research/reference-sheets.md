# Owner's reference sheets: what each teaches (described for agents that cannot see images)

Technique notes only; the art belongs to its authors and is not copied into the films.

## Motion and posing
| Sheet | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Samurai movement sheet (5 numbered poses) | 1 "Full extension, power comes from the ground up" (overhead jodan, back leg straight, front knee bent). 2 "Low dodge, keep head out of line" (body nearly flat, one hand on the ground). 3 "Explosive drive, linear power" (lunge, blade level, back leg straight, body one diagonal line). 4 "Spin off hip, look over shoulder, commit" (spin with the head leading). 5 "Calm before the storm" (wide low stance, weight centred low, blade low). Caption: "No waste. Every motion has a purpose. Efficiency is lethal." |
| Force-line diagram (Japanese notes) | The stepping foot's force goes to the centre of gravity; the bracing foot's force goes to the upper-body centre of mass; their sum is the line of action. Big cuts = one long curved line of action from the back foot through the hips to the tip. |
| Orange kung-fu breakdown (frames 0–178) | Numbered keys with holds ("10–15 hold of the body with variation of the hands"), "extreme shoulder twist", "weight goes to this leg", contact frames marked, jumps with "high position", squash on landing ("squash and momentum"), arcs drawn for hands/feet. Uneven spacing: contact and high points get keys, the rest are breakdowns. |
| Game animation key sheets (Korean notes) | Each attack = anticipation pose, 1–2 smear frames with a red arc, contact, overshoot, recovery; red arcs show the tip path; blue lines show the body's travel and ground marks. |
| Gesture sheets ("Gesturefest", grey capoeira poses, dynamic low poses) | Line of action first: every pose is one clear curve (C or S); extreme silhouettes read even as stick figures. Low wide stances, hands touching the ground, cartwheels, kicks. |
| Lunge sketches (spear/sword), green Japanese "basic continuous motion" (pose 4 → 5 "swing through to pose 5 in one go; mind the blade's path") | Full-length lunges with the back leg straight and the blade continuing the leg's line; cut "in one go" with attention to the blade trajectory. |
| Zsethh / hooded swordsman sketches | Long coat as a big trailing shape; glowing blade; the coat finishes moving after the body. |
| Two-sword stance sketch | Very wide low stance, arms out with two blades; mannequin boxes for torso/pelvis/limbs (our rig is the same idea). |

## Sword craft
| Sheet | Lesson |
|---|---|
| "Sens de la coupe" | 8 cut directions around a clock: 1 straight down, 2/3 diagonal down (kesa / gyaku-kesa), 4/5 horizontal (both ways), 6/7 rising diagonals, 8 thrust. |
| Iaido kata cut lines (ZNKR seitei 1–12: Mae, Ushiro, Uke-nagashi, Tsuka-ate, Kesa-giri, Morote-tsuki, Sanpo-giri, Ganmen-ate, Soete-tsuki, Shiho-giri, So-giri, Nuki-uchi) | Exact lines on the body: kiri-oroshi straight down the centre, temple-height horizontals (komekami), diagonal kesa from shoulder to opposite hip, thrusts to the solar plexus (suigetsu). Use these as named target lines for cuts. |
| Grip diagram (te-no-uchi) | Blade on the centre line, left hand drives, small wrist turn at the end (our `rwz` snap). |
| Manga iai insert panels | Close-ups for the draw: hand settles on the tsuka, thumb pushes the tsuba (koiguchi o kiru), extreme close-up of the guard leaving the scabbard. 3 quick inserts before the draw. |
| Glowing purple blade drawn from the scabbard (3 panels) | The demonic blade's glow is revealed inch by inch from the saya mouth, then a white speed-line burst on the draw. |

## Effects
| Sheet | Lesson |
|---|---|
| Staff "weakening charge" (whirl, corkscrew slam, lunge kick, rapid thrusts) | Smears are solid grey crescent SHAPES, wide at the leading edge and tapering, not thin lines; rapid thrusts = 3–5 overlapping ghost blades with straight speed streaks; a slam ends in a red jagged ground burst. |
| Explosion shapes (blue line sheet) | Six silhouettes: mushroom with a ring, puffy cloud stack, spike burst, curling crown splash, jagged flame splash, low dust cloud. All start with a sharp vertical spike, then round off. |
| Cube impact sheet | Impact = white star burst with radial spikes + shards flying + a straight motion trail behind the projectile + ground ripples. |
| Ice magic (growing / firing / exploding) | Three-phase effect: charge (small swirl + crystals), release (long ribbon trail), impact (radial streaks and shards). |
| Fire pillar sheet | Silhouette characters + bright effect; ground cracks, rings around a vertical pillar, dark smoke balls behind, white ground splashes. |
| Red slash arc on black | The slash: white-hot core, crimson outer glow, thin trailing filaments, fades along its length. (Our demonic slash.) |
| Blue orbit rings, blue light lines | Energy as glowing ellipses around a bright point; crossing light beams with sparks. |
| Blue sword smears on silhouettes | Smears in cool blue-violet over black silhouettes; ground dust wakes under every dash. |
