# Story bible: 紙の剣 · PAPER CINEMA

A series of short sword-duel films in the Lantern Origami style: real duels and battles from Japanese history,
each told with one mythic thread, the cursed swords. **History is marked honestly** (see `research/`).
**The cursed swords are our fiction** and are always presented as legend.

## The world's rules (never break these)
1. **Light is life.** Every paper being glows amber from inside. When a character dies or a story ends, a lantern goes out.
2. **Crimson is the curse.** Demonic power glows crimson through the paper seams. The stronger the curse, the more it leaks.
3. **Paper, not flesh.** Cuts split paper and spill red paint (shu-iro vermilion), never blood. Paper shards fly on every hit.
4. **The ink world is memory.** Black-and-white sumi-e ink scenes are the past: legends, flashbacks and origins.
5. **Every film opens on the paper sea** (the lantern-wave opening) and **ends on the end card** with the next episode.

## Recurring characters

| Character | Status | Look (library preset) | Notes |

| Miyamoto Musashi | Historical (1584–1645) | dark indigo kosode, brown hakama, white headband (`musashi`) | Two-sword school (Niten Ichi-ryū). Wrote *Gorin no Sho*. Wooden sword at Ganryūjima (tradition). |
| Sasaki Kojirō | Historical, many details tradition | cream kosode, crimson haori (`kojiro`) | "Monohoshizao" long sword (tradition). Ganryū school. |
| The Wandering Ronin | Fiction, our hero | straw-hat folded-paper samurai (your base image, `ronin`) | Carries **Kurenai-oni**. Never speaks. His interior light flickers when the blade hungers. |
| The Kimono Swordswoman | Fiction | red and teal kimono, red armour plates (TRELLIS model) | Hunts cursed blades and seals them. |
| The Gold Armoured Samurai | Fiction | gold origami armour (TRELLIS model) | A general whose armour is folded from old prayers. |

## The cursed swords (fiction, in `library/swords/`)

| Sword | Glow | Legend (our fiction) |
|---|---|---|
| **Kurenai-oni 紅鬼** (crimson demon) | crimson | Forged with ink mixed from a demon's paper heart. It cuts the light out of whatever it touches. |
| **Yamikaze 闇風** (dark wind) | violet | It moves before its wielder does. Owners are always one step early, and eventually one step into the grave. |
| **Shiokaze 潮風** (sea wind) | none | Not cursed: the honest blade. It appears where a curse must be broken. |
| **Byakko 白虎** (white tiger) | none | A temple guardian's sword that refuses to be drawn in anger. |

## Tone
- Silence before steel: long holds, then sudden violence (Strike Grammar).
- No gore, no cruelty for its own sake. Death is shown as light going out.
- Respect for the historical people: tradition is labelled as tradition on screen.
