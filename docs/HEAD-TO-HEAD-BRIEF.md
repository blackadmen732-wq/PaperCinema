# Head-to-head brief (give this, word for word, to every environment)

Make a 15-second vertical (9:16, 24 fps) sword-duel scene with two fighters. It must contain: a
standoff, a clash exchange, a dodge, and a decisive cut; at least three camera changes, one of them a
hard cut on the impact; one destruction beat (a prop or a paper panel is cut and falls); and sound:
a whoosh before every strike, a clash on every blade contact, an impact on the cut, and a bed under
all of it. Deliver one MP4 with sound at output/head-to-head.mp4 and a cue list (time and name of
every sound) at output/head-to-head.cues.json. Work without asking questions. Stop when it is
delivered or when 2 hours have passed.
