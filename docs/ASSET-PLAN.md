# Openings and the 3D asset plan (Mark, Nov 26)

Two questions from Mark on 26 Nov, answered once here so no session has to ask again:
1. *How does a video start?* — and should his three reference images (a dragon-guard katana in white, an oni mask on
   chains with a 鬼神 tag, a blade through a flower) be the opening?
2. *If the studio gets an AI 3D-generation API, what will it make, part by part, and what analysis decides it?*

## 1. How every video starts (the first 3 seconds)

The rule this project already has (`docs/DIRECTION.md` §Shorts: "Hook in 1 s. Viewers decide in about the first
second"; `docs/EFFECTS-PLAN.md`: "a 3-second Shorts opening, hook first, title second"). The signature opening in
`library/openings/lantern-wave.json` is **6 s** of fly-over and title *before* the story: on a Short that is the
part people swipe past. So the order is:

| time | what | why |
|---|---|---|
| 0.00–0.15 s | the sound first (a blade ring or one taiko hit) over the first lit frame | sound pulls the eye before the picture lands |
| 0.15–1.2 s | **the cold-open prop**: one extreme close-up, ONE strong motion (below) | a striking, moving image in second 1 |
| 1.2–3.0 s | the hook line on screen over the story's first picture ("1612. He was late on purpose.") | the question the viewer stays for |
| ~3.0 s | the 紙の剣 seal: a small red hanko-style stamp in a corner for ~1 s while the story runs | the brand, without stopping the story |
| end | the end card that exists (`library/endcards/paper-cinema.json`, 2.6 s: 紙の剣 / PAPER CINEMA / next episode) | |

The lantern-wave fly-over is cut to ~1.5 s and used as the way INTO the story world after the hook, or dropped.

## 2. Mark's three images → three cold-open props (rebuilt as ours, in paper)

The images are references, not footage: they look like downloaded art and photos, so they are **not put in a video
as they are** (rights unknown, and a found image in a monetised Short can be claimed). Each becomes our own paper 3D
prop, which also makes it match the films. One opening per video, rotated by story type:

| | reference | the opening shot | use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | dragon-guard katana, white and black | **The glint.** Extreme close-up on a paper katana's dragon tsuba; the blade slides out a hand's width; a white glint sweeps across the guard; hard cut on the "shing". | duels (Episode 1) |
| B | oni mask on chains, 鬼神 tag, kunai | **The curse wakes.** The mask hangs in the dark, swaying on its cord; on a low drum its eye holes ignite crimson (the story bible: crimson = the curse); the 鬼神 tag turns to camera. | cursed-sword episodes |
| C | a blade through a flower, ink flowing | **The petal cut.** A blade drops point-first through a paper flower, which splits into petals that drift away in ink. Use a sakura blossom or our own crest (mon), **not a four-leaf clover** — a clover with a sword reads as fan art of the anime *Black Clover*. | loss, love, tragedy |

The white katana image with big black kanji is the right look for the **thumbnail template, the channel banner and
the profile art** (white paper, black ink = the brand), not for second 0 of a video: a still title card is the most
swiped opening there is. Spelling: the end card already uses **紙の剣** (*kami no tsurugi*, "paper sword"), three bold
characters; **紙の映画** (*kami no eiga*) is the literal "Paper Cinema". Use ONE everywhere.

## 3. What gets generated and what does not (`.claude/skills/buy-the-geometry/SKILL.md`)

| generated by an API (worth credits) | built here (never generated) |
|---|---|
| rigid things: masks, helmets, hats, armour plates, swords, guards, scabbards, props, hero buildings | cloth that bends (kimono, hakama, sleeves: `part_registry.py`), the skeleton, poses, fights, camera, effects (slash, shards, SHIMI paint), sky, water |

A whole generated character is one fused shell whose arms cannot bend (why `rig_trellis.py` was abandoned). A
generated PART is fine: one rigid thing on one bone. `tools/gen3d.mjs` (Tripo or Meshy, text or image → GLB, or
segment a GLB into parts) already exists; `reg.asset_part()` turns any GLB into a paper part on a socket.

## 4. The analysis before any credit is spent (per asset)

1. **Script → shot list → noun list.** Every object a line names or a shot shows ("show the word"). For each: which
   shots, how close (wide / medium / close / extreme close), how long on screen, does it move, does it bend, who
   holds it.
2. **Reuse check.** Is it already in the kit or the parts (pines, rocks, torii, stone lanterns, bridge, castle,
   dojo, bamboo, the boat, the bokken)? Then nothing is generated.
3. **Classify.** Rigid hero prop seen close → generate. Rigid background prop → kit first. Cloth or body → fold in
   code. Effect → procedural.
4. **A spec sheet.** Real size in cm; the history or lore it must respect (`research/`, confidence marks); the style
   (folded washi, visible facets, amber inner light, crimson only for the demonic); paper sheets from
   `library/paper-materials/`; where it attaches (socket and bone, or a set mark); face budget (~900 for a part,
   more for an extreme close-up); the reference image and the prompt.
5. **Generate 2–4 candidates**, judge them as turnarounds in our light and from the real shot camera; reject melted
   geometry, wrong scale, a plastic look.
6. **Integrate:** normalise origin, scale and orientation, decimate to paper planes, paper material,
   `asset_part()`, the part validator, a turnaround, the shot still.
7. **Into the library** with its data (size, sockets, prompt, service, cost, licence/credit), so every later episode
   uses it by name: generated once, reused forever.
8. **A cost line** per asset in the spend log, inside the budget Mark sets.

## 5. Everything, by section (priority order)

1. **Brand and openings:** the dragon-tsuba katana (A), the oni mask set with cords, chains, tassels, the 鬼神 tag and
   a kunai (B), the blade and paper flower or crest (C), the channel crest (mon) and the red hanko seal.
2. **Weapons:** Kojirō's *Monohoshizao* (blade ~91 cm, tradition) with an ornate tsuba and its scabbard (he throws it
   into the sea); katana tsuba variants (dragon, wave, crest, plain iron); wakizashi; tantō; saya with sageo cords and
   tassels; naginata; yari; yumi and arrows; kunai and shuriken.
3. **Character parts (rigid only):** masks (oni, hannya, kitsune, the paper-mask variants), kabuto with crests,
   kasa and jingasa, armour plates (dō, sode, kote, suneate), headband variants, hair pieces, sandals.
4. **Props:** paper lanterns (chōchin: the lantern that goes out when a character dies, story bible rule 1), stone
   lanterns, nobori banners with crests, scrolls, letters, sake jar and cups, a tea set, coins, a shrine bell,
   incense, a bonsai pine.
5. **Buildings, hero pieces only:** shrine hall, pagoda, castle gate, teahouse, village houses (the kit covers
   walls, roofs, gates, torii and bridges).
6. **Nature hero pieces:** procedural first (pine, cherry, bamboo, rocks already exist).

## 7. To connect a service
- Mark picks **Tripo** or **Meshy**; its key goes in through the secure form (`request_credential`), never in chat
  or in a file, and the host (`openapi.tripo3d.ai` or `api.meshy.ai`) is added to the environment.
- **Check that the plan allows commercial use** before a model goes into a posted video; some tiers do not, or
  require a credit line.
- A monthly credit budget; the cost of the first model is measured and shown before more are made.
